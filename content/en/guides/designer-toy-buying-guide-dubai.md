## Choose for the person, not only the character

A collector may want a named series or a particular character. A gift buyer needs to know whether the recipient prefers a display piece or something to carry. A parent also needs the exact age and safety information. Share who the piece is for and how it will be used when you [browse our collection](/products/).

## Make the format clear before comparing prices

**Blind box:** establish whether the offer is a sealed random box, a known character or a set. Check the included pieces and the seller's description of the format.

**Display figure:** check the actual dimensions, condition, included accessories and the place where it will stand. Product photographs help identify the character; dimensions help decide whether it fits.

**Plush keychain or hanging accessory:** check the attachment and size for the intended bag or display. Ask for material and care information, including any rigid details or detachable components.

Our catalogue includes DaiXiaoDai Animal Carnival figures and keychain variants. Use the exact variant name in your enquiry so the figure and its keychain version are not treated as the same item.

## Ask about materials and the experience of using it

Compare the surface, soft and rigid components, attachment, packaging and care requirements. A product's name or appearance does not establish its material composition, washability or suitability for a child. Ask for those details on the chosen item.

For a desk, think about display space and handling. For a bag, think about attachment and regular wear. For a gift, think about the opening experience and whether the recipient wants a surprise or a specific character.

## Buying one gift and commissioning a brand project

For an existing collectible, confirm the variant, package contents, condition, quantity and delivery terms.

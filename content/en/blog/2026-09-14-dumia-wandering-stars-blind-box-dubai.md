## Display and Care in the Gulf Climate

The intense UV exposure and temperature fluctuations characteristic of the Gulf climate require thoughtful display strategies. Here are practical considerations for preserving your Wandering Stars collection:

- **Avoid direct sunlight.** Position display shelves away from windows, or use UV-filtering glass cases. The deep indigo and silver tones in the Wandering Stars palette are particularly susceptible to UV fading.
- **Control humidity.** Air-conditioned display environments are ideal. Avoid placing figures in areas prone to moisture, such as near kitchen areas or bathrooms.
- **Dust regularly.** The vinyl-plush hybrid construction traps dust in plush areas. A soft-bristle brush or compressed air canister removes buildup without damaging surfaces.
- **Rotate displays.** If you have a large collection, consider cycling figures in and out of primary display. This reduces cumulative light exposure while keeping your setup feeling fresh.

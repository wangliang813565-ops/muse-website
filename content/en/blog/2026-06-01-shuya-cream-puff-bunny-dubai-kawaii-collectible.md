## How to Display Your SHUYA Collection

A collection worth building is a collection worth displaying. Here are three approaches that work particularly well with the Cream Puff Bunny aesthetic.

**The Pastry Shop Shelf.** Arrange your SHUYA figures on a single tiered shelf with actual dessert props — a small ceramic cake stand, a miniature macaron display. The visual pun is deliberate and delightful. Warm LED strip lighting in the 3000K range keeps the pastel colours reading true.

**The Gradient Lineup.** If you have collected multiple variants, arrange them by colour temperature: warm tones on the left, cooling to pastel blues and greens on the right. This turns a random assortment into an intentional gradient that rewards the eye.

**The Companion Pairing.** Place a 12CM Joy figure next to its 7CM Mood counterpart. The scale contrast creates visual interest, and guests will immediately understand that these belong to the same world.

Whatever display approach you choose, keep direct sunlight off the figures. Vinyl and plush both fade over time under UV exposure, and pastel colours are particularly vulnerable.

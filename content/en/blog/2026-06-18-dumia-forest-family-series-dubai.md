## A Family That Lives in the Woods

The Dumia universe has always been about storytelling — each series a new chapter, each character a distinct personality woven into a larger narrative. With the **Forest Family Series**, Maytree takes a gentler turn. Instead of galactic adventures or enchanted tea parties, this collection asks a simpler question: what does it mean to belong?

The Forest Family Series features a cast of woodland-inspired vinyl plush figures, each designed as part of an extended forest-dwelling family. There is the wise elder raccoon who carries a lantern through twilight groves, the curious fox cub clutching an acorn twice the size of its paw, and the gentle bear mother whose expression alone tells you she has seen many seasons pass. These are not standalone collectibles — they are characters that live in relation to one another, and that is precisely what makes this series feel different.

## The Craft of Vinyl Plush

For collectors unfamiliar with the format, Maytree's vinyl plush figures occupy a sweet spot between traditional PVC figures and soft plush toys. The outer shell is smooth vinyl with a subtle matte finish that catches light beautifully on a display shelf — no glossy glare, no fingerprint magnets. Inside, plush stuffing gives each figure a satisfying weight and a slight give when held. It is a tactile experience that photographs can only hint at.

The Forest Family figures stand approximately 8 to 10 centimetres tall, making them ideal for desk displays, bookshelf arrangements, and the kind of curated shelfie that performs well on Instagram. Each blind box contains one random character from the series, with a few secret chase variants that seasoned Maytree collectors will already be hunting for.

## Why "Family" Resonates in GCC

There is a particular reason the Forest Family concept lands well with collectors in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and the wider Gulf. Family — in Arabic, *al-ailah* — is not a background theme here. It is the organising principle of social life. Weekend gatherings, intergenerational homes, the ritual of evening coffee with cousins — these are not special occasions but the texture of everyday life.

A blind box series that celebrates family bonds, then, is not just cute. It is culturally legible in a way that more esoteric designer toy themes sometimes are not. A collector in Jeddah or Doha might look at the bear mother figure and see something familiar. The fox cub might remind someone of a youngest sibling. This is the quiet genius of the Forest Family Series: it connects without trying too hard.

## Display Ideas for the Forest Family

Because these figures are vinyl plush rather than rigid plastic, they work exceptionally well in mixed-media displays. Here are three approaches that MUSE collectors have been experimenting with:

1. **The Woodland Shelf** — Layer dried moss, small pinecones, and warm LED fairy lights behind your Forest Family figures. The matte vinyl finish contrasts beautifully with organic textures.

2. **The Family Portrait** — Arrange all pulled figures in a loose semicircle facing forward, as if posing for a family photo. The varied heights and poses create natural visual rhythm.

3. **The Story Box** — Place a single figure inside a small glass terrarium with a mini mushroom or acorn prop. It transforms the blind box toy into a tiny diorama that guests will lean in to examine.

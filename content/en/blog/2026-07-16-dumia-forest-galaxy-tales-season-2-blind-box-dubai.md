## The Galaxy-Touched Forest Gets Bigger

When the first Dumia Forest Galaxy Tales collection launched, it established something unusual in the designer toy world: a single IP built across three distinct formats from day one. The vinyl plush blind box, the movable vinyl plush edition, and the crossbody bag each carved out their own space on collector shelves across Dubai and the wider GCC. If you followed the original series, you will remember the midnight blues, the scattered starlight patterns, and the luminous colour palette that made every figure glow differently depending on where and how it was displayed.

Now the forest is growing again. **Dumia Forest Galaxy Tales Season 2** (SKU HX41100) brings a fresh wave of characters into the galaxy-touched universe, and while it builds on everything the first season established, it introduces enough new visual territory to feel like a genuine expansion rather than a simple re-skin. For collectors who built a Season 1 shelf and thought they had the full picture, this is the moment that picture gets more interesting.

## What Changes in Season 2

Season 2 of the Galaxy Tales series arrives in the core vinyl plush blind box format at seventeen centimetres — the same size as the original blind box that anchored Season 1. This continuity is deliberate. It means Season 2 figures sit naturally alongside their predecessors on the same shelf, creating a seamless visual narrative across both waves without any awkward size mismatches or jarring scale shifts.

Where Season 2 diverges from its predecessor is in character design and colour theory. The original series leaned heavily into deep celestial tones — midnight blues, indigo, and the occasional flash of silver starlight. Season 2 broadens the palette. Expect warmer cosmic hues, the kind of tones you see when a nebula catches infrared light. There are more amber and coral accents, more gradient transitions between deep space and forest floor, and a broader range of fabric finishes that give individual characters a distinct tactile identity within the collection.

The character designs also push further into the crossover concept that gives Galaxy Tales its name. Season 1 characters felt like forest creatures wearing starlight. Season 2 characters feel like the forest and the galaxy have genuinely merged — creatures that seem to grow from the boundary between earth and cosmos. It is a subtle but important distinction. Season 1 said, "These creatures live in a galaxy-touched forest." Season 2 says, "These creatures are made of it."

## The Blind Box Format: Why It Still Works

The blind box format remains the beating heart of the Galaxy Tales collection, and there is a reason Maytree keeps it as the primary entry point. At seventeen centimetres, each figure is large enough to showcase intricate fabric detail — the starlight embroidery, the gradient dye work, the small constellation patterns on individual characters — but compact enough that a full set does not dominate a room. A complete Season 2 collection fits on a single shelf without requiring the kind of display real estate that larger vinyl figures demand.

For collectors in Dubai apartments and Dubai villas alike, this scale balance matters. The blind box format also preserves the core pleasure of the collecting experience: the surprise. Each box in the series conceals a different character, and the unboxing moment — when a collector discovers whether they have pulled the one they were hunting for — is an experience that digital purchasing has not managed to replicate. In a market that increasingly moves online, the physical blind box unboxing remains a ritual that collectors protect.

Season 2 also appears to sharpen the probability design behind the character distribution. While the specific lineup remains part of the reveal, the series is structured to give collectors a realistic chance of building a complete set through standard retail purchases rather than requiring the kind of speculative buying that makes some blind box lines feel punishingly opaque. This is consistent with Maytree's approach to the original series, where set completion was achievable for dedicated collectors.

## Building on Season 1: For New and Existing Collectors

If you are new to the Galaxy Tales universe, Season 2 is a natural entry point. The characters are self-contained enough to enjoy on their own merits, and the visual storytelling does not require knowledge of Season 1 to appreciate. You do not need to have opened a single Season 1 box to understand what makes a Season 2 figure special — the craftsmanship, the design detail, and the galaxy-forest crossover concept all stand independently.

For collectors who built a Season 1 collection, the relationship between the two seasons becomes part of the appeal. Season 2 figures placed alongside their Season 1 counterparts create a visual timeline — a shelf that tells the story of a universe expanding. The colour palette shift from Season 1's cooler celestial tones to Season 2's warmer nebula accents means the combined display has more visual range than either season alone. It is the kind of evolution that rewards long-term collecting without making newcomers feel like they missed out.

## 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to **MUSE Information Services L.L.C-FZ**, registered in **Meydan Free Zone, Dubai, UAE**, operating the consumer-facing brand **MUSE Trendy Toy** at https://toys.aimuse.ae/ (the "Site").

When this policy says "we," "us," or "MUSE," it refers to the company above.

## 2. Scope

This policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect Personal Data that we obtain through:

- This Site (toys.aimuse.ae) and any subdomain
- WhatsApp business inquiries to +971 55 567 1672
- Email correspondence with our business address
- Order forms, custom-gift briefs, and IP-collaboration proposals you submit to us
- Trade-show meetings, in-person visits to our Dubai warehouse, and B2B retailer onboarding

We comply with the **UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data ("UAE PDPL")** and the Executive Regulations issued under it.

## 3. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data needed to fulfill orders and operate the business. Specifically:

- **Identity & contact data:** name, job title, business email, mobile/WhatsApp number, postal address (for delivery)
- **Business data:** company name, trade licence number (if you are a registered retailer or corporate gift buyer), VAT registration number, payment terms agreed
- **Order data:** product selection, quantities, customisation specs, delivery instructions
- **Communication data:** WhatsApp messages, email threads, meeting notes, design briefs
- **Site analytics (minimal):** standard HTTP request logs (IP address, user-agent, referrer) collected by GitHub Pages / Cloudflare infrastructure. We do not run third-party tracking or advertising cookies on this Site.

We do not knowingly collect special-category data (health, religion, ethnicity, biometric, financial credentials). Please do not send such information to us.

## 4. How We Use Your Data

We process Personal Data only for the following purposes:

1. **Contract performance** — quoting, invoicing, fulfilling, delivering, and supporting your orders
2. **Customer service** — responding to inquiries, complaints, and warranty/exchange requests
3. **Legitimate business interests** — supplier coordination, logistics planning, fraud prevention
4. **Direct marketing with consent** — only if you explicitly opt-in to receive product announcements (you may opt out at any time via reply-STOP on WhatsApp or unsubscribe link in email)
5. **Legal compliance** — VAT records, customs declarations, UAE tax authority reporting

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing (UAE PDPL Art. 4–5)

- **Performance of a contract** (you ordered from us)
- **Legitimate interests** that do not override your rights (running our distribution business)
- **Consent** (only for marketing communications, separately and clearly obtained)
- **Legal obligation** (UAE Commercial Companies Law, tax law, customs)

## 6. Who We Share Data With

We share Personal Data only when necessary:

- **Logistics partners** — Aramex, DHL, Emirates Post (for GCC delivery)
- **Payment processors** — UAE banks or processors handling your invoice payment (we do not store your card details ourselves)
- **Manufacturers and suppliers** — only the minimum needed to fulfil your custom or co-branded order
- **Professional advisors** — auditors, legal counsel, bound by confidentiality
- **Authorities** — when legally required (court order, tax audit, customs inspection)

**We never sell your data to third parties for advertising.**

## 7. International Data Transfers

To deliver custom designer toys we transfer order data to manufacturers in **China** and elsewhere. Under UAE PDPL Article 22, we transfer only when:

- The receiving country has adequate protection, OR
- The transfer is necessary to perform a contract you requested, OR
- You have given explicit consent

We use confidentiality clauses with all supplier partners.

## 8. How Long We Keep Data

| Data type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Invoices, customs records | **7 years** (UAE Commercial Companies Law) |
| Active customer files | Duration of business relationship + 3 years |
| One-off inquiries (no order) | 24 months |
| Marketing consent records | Until you withdraw consent + 1 year |

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

## 9. Your Rights Under UAE PDPL

You have the right to:

- **Access** the Personal Data we hold about you
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erase** your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Restrict** processing in certain circumstances
- **Portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** for any marketing communications

## 10. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any right, or to submit a **Data Subject Access Request (DSAR)**, email **leon@evplus.ae** with:

1. The right you are exercising (access / rectification / erasure / etc.)
2. Sufficient information for us to identify you and your records
3. A copy of your Emirates ID or equivalent ID (we redact it after verification)

We respond within **30 days** as required by UAE PDPL.

## 11. Cookies & Tracking

This Site is a Hugo static site hosted on GitHub Pages. **We do not set advertising cookies, behavioural-tracking cookies, or third-party marketing pixels.** Standard HTTP server logs (kept by GitHub Pages / Cloudflare infrastructure) include IP and user-agent; these are operational logs only.

## 12. Children

Our Site and services are aimed at adult B2B buyers and adult consumers. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under **18**. If you believe a minor has provided us data, email leon@evplus.ae and we will delete it.

## 13. Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organisational measures: HTTPS-only transit, access controls on customer files, encryption of payment correspondence, no card data stored locally.

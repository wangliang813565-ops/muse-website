## Who We Are

**MUSE Trendy Toy** (operated by **MUSE Information Services L.L.C-FZ**, registered in **Meydan Free Zone, Dubai**) is the GCC's first professional designer toy distributor. Founded in 2024 by **Leon Wang (王亮)**, MUSE bridges the gap between China's vast designer toy supply chain and Middle East retail demand — making it possible for GCC retailers, corporate buyers, and collectors to access trending collectibles without the 25-35 day overseas import wait.

We operate out of a Dubai warehouse with **2-5 day delivery across all 6 GCC countries** — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Oman. Our catalog spans **50+ SKUs** from 10+ top Chinese designer toy brands, plus custom-branded corporate merchandise for Ramadan, Eid, UAE National Day, and corporate occasions.

## How We Got Here

MUSE was built on a thesis: the Middle East's appetite for designer toys, blind boxes, and original character IP was already proven by Pop Mart's GCC expansion — but the market lacked a **local distributor who actually understood both ends of the supply chain**.

Founder **Leon Wang** spent 6 years inside one of China's Top-5 talent agencies (**Huaxing Cuican / 华星璀璨**, 2017-2023), where he ran a 130-person MCN business across gaming live-streams, entertainment IP, and creator-talent signings (signed IPs include Nailong, Xiao Yu Gaming, Tang Ren Xiao Ge, Yi Mo, with 200+ million-follower-class creators on roster). He then founded **Taleo**, an overseas short-drama app, building it from 0-to-1 across content sourcing, production, and localization. That dual-DNA — **big-pool China operator × overseas 0-to-1 founder** — became the operating system for MUSE.

In late 2024, Leon moved to Dubai and incorporated **MUSE Information Services L.L.C-FZ** in **Meydan Free Zone**, secured exclusive commercial rights for top Middle Eastern KOLs (most notably Aboflah, 49M+ YouTube subscribers — #1 creator in the Middle East), and built MUSE's first warehouse and distribution network.

## What We Do

### 1. Designer Toy Distribution (Core Business)

We import 50+ SKUs from **China's top designer toy brands** to our Dubai warehouse:

- **Daimio** — animal carnival keychain series, custom plush
- **Maytree** — zodiac blind boxes, premium collector editions
- **Dumia** — Aria Snow Whisper editorial collection, art-toy crossovers
- 10+ other emerging Asian designer toy houses

GCC retailers, gift shops, and collectibles outlets stock from us — getting **2-5 day delivery** instead of 25-35 day overseas freight, **flexible MOQ** instead of brand-direct minimums, and **GCC-curated selection** instead of generic global catalogs.

### 2. Corporate Gifting

MUSE's custom branded merchandise covers the Middle East seasonal calendar:

- **Ramadan** themed gift sets — branded dates packaging, character plushies, premium kits
- **Eid Al-Fitr / Eid Al-Adha** corporate gifts
- **UAE National Day** (Dec 2) and **GCC National Days** — nationalist-themed collectibles
- **Corporate events** — branded conference giveaways, anniversary commemoratives, milestone gifts

Active B2B partners include HMG Group, Dr.O Group Holding, Gogo Village, and Geekay Distribution.

### 3. Hala IP Licensing

**Hala** is MUSE's original IP — a baby Saker Falcon from Abu Dhabi, reimagined as a cozy character in traditional Emirati dress. The Saker Falcon (Falco cherrug) is the UAE's national bird and a symbol of Bedouin tradition. Hala embodies Gulf hospitality: warm, proud, and quietly healing.

Hala is available for IP licensing collaboration with brands targeting GCC markets — co-branded blind boxes, custom merchandise series, retail activations, and event-specific character drops. Protected by ADiP (Abu Dhabi IP) patent and trademark infrastructure.

### 4. MENA × China IP Co-Branding

Via Leon's Aboflah Greater-China commercial agency rights and government cultural-tourism partnerships, MUSE arranges cross-border IP collaborations between Middle East top-tier KOLs and Chinese supply-chain partners — turning short-form viral moments into licensed products.

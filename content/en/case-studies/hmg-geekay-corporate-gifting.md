## Project Overview

**Partners**: [HMG Group](https://hmggroup.com) (UAE retail-distribution conglomerate) and Geekay Distribution (regional toy and lifestyle wholesaler).

**Project Type**: Multi-occasion branded merchandise programme — Ramadan/Eid gift sets, UAE National Day commemoratives, corporate event giveaways, and seasonal retail collections.

**Scope**: Concept → Chinese supplier coordination → ESMA conformity (children's items) → UAE customs → distribution across HMG and Geekay's retail networks.

**Year**: 2024–present (ongoing).

## Background

HMG Group and Geekay Distribution serve thousands of GCC retail points: gift shops, mall kiosks, hotel souvenir corners, school stationery aisles, and corporate-gifting B2B clients. The retail calendar in the GCC is heavily seasonal:

- **Ramadan & Eid Al-Fitr** (spring/early summer) — gift-giving peak; themed packaging, dates collectibles, premium gift sets
- **Eid Al-Adha** (autumn) — second gift season
- **UAE National Day (Dec 2)** & GCC National Days — nationalist-themed collectibles, branded keychains, flag-motif merchandise
- **Year-round corporate events** — conference giveaways, anniversary commemoratives, milestone gifts

The challenge for HMG/Geekay: each season demands fresh designs, but MOQ from Chinese manufacturers is 500–2,000 units and lead times are 6+ weeks. Without a local distribution layer that holds ready stock, they end up shipping orders late or overcommitting on inventory.

## What MUSE Delivered

### 1. Catalog Coordination

Per quarter, we pre-design and pre-source 20+ SKUs across:

- Branded keychains (acrylic, metal, plush)
- Themed packaging (Ramadan dates boxes, Eid greeting kits, National Day flag motifs)
- Branded apparel (caps, t-shirts, scarves)
- Limited-edition collectibles (small plush, vinyl figures with cultural motifs)

Quarterly catalog goes to HMG/Geekay's retail and B2B-gifting teams for SKU selection.

### 2. China Manufacturer Network

MUSE coordinates production across our supplier network in Guangdong and Zhejiang:

- **Daimio** (real B2B partner) — custom plush and keychain capability
- **Maytree** — blind boxes and premium-edition runs
- 30+ specialty manufacturers for branded packaging, apparel, and limited collectibles

MOQ is split across our network — HMG/Geekay can order 100 units of one SKU + 100 of another, rather than committing to 1,000 of a single design from a single factory.

### 3. ESMA Conformity (Children's Items)

For items meeting the children's-product classification (sub-15 cm plush, anything age-recommended below 15), we run **EQM conformity** in parallel with production. Standard 2–4 week timeline.

For adult-collector or corporate-gift items (clearly age-15+ marketing), conformity is optional but documented for customs review.

### 4. Customs & VAT Flow

MUSE handles import through our cleared brokers:

- **HS 9503** for toys/collectibles, **HS 9505** for festive/carnival items
- 5% UAE customs duty on FOB ([UAE Federal Customs](https://www.fca.gov.ae))
- 5% VAT on CIF+duty ([UAE Federal Tax Authority](https://tax.gov.ae))
- Input-VAT recovery flows through MUSE TRN, passed to retailers VAT-compliant

### 5. Distribution to HMG & Geekay Retail Footprint

From MUSE Dubai warehouse:

- HMG retail network (UAE-wide): 1–2 day delivery via Aramex
- Geekay regional distribution (UAE + KSA + Oman): 2–4 days
- Pick-pack accuracy critical — each batch invoiced separately, traceable per retail-point

## Outcome

- **Quarterly catalog rhythm** established (4 seasonal drops per year)
- **MOQ flexibility** unlocked smaller pilots — HMG can test new SKUs with 100-unit orders rather than 1,000-unit commitments
- **Lead time compressed** from 6–10 weeks (China-direct) to **3–5 weeks** (MUSE-coordinated, hold inventory for replenishment)
- **Ramadan 2025** & **UAE National Day 2024** campaigns delivered on-schedule via MUSE supply chain

## Why It Worked

**1. Seasonal cadence + ready stock combination.** HMG/Geekay didn't have to forecast seasonal demand 4 months in advance from China. MUSE's Dubai warehouse held inventory across our catalog, with rapid replenishment for breakout SKUs.

**2. China-side direct relationships.** MUSE's Aboflah-team-trained operations (born of Leon Wang's Huaxing Cuican / 华星璀璨 background) translate Chinese-language SKU specs and quality-control into UAE-shippable product without translation loss.

**3. MOQ flexibility unlocked smaller test runs.** This is what HMG and Geekay teams specifically valued — being able to test new designs without 1,000-unit commitment per SKU.

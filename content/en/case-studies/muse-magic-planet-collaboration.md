## Project Overview

**Partner**: Magic Planet, the family-entertainment retail brand operated by **[Majid Al Futtaim Entertainment](https://www.majidalfuttaim.com)** across UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, and Bahrain shopping malls.

**Project Type**: Original-IP designer toy collaboration — custom blind-box and plush series for in-park retail kiosks across Magic Planet locations.

**Scope**: Concept design → ADiP IP protection → manufacturer sourcing → ESMA conformity → in-park retail rollout.

**Year**: 2024–2025 (active).

## Background

Magic Planet operates 30+ family-entertainment centres across GCC shopping malls (Mall of the Emirates, City Centre Mirdif, Mall of Egypt, and more). The brand has strong foot traffic — families spending 60–120 minutes per visit, with a defined retail conversion window inside each park.

The opportunity: introduce a **GCC-themed designer toy IP** that families could collect as keepsakes from their Magic Planet visits — turning a one-time entertainment experience into a continuing collectible relationship.

## What MUSE Delivered

### 1. Concept & IP Development

We proposed several Gulf-heritage character concepts. The final selection became **Hala**, a baby Saker Falcon character inspired by the UAE's national bird tradition, designed as a cozy collectible in traditional Emirati dress.

MUSE registered Hala under **ADiP (Abu Dhabi IP)** patent and trademark infrastructure to ensure clean IP ownership before retail rollout.

### 2. Co-Branded Collection Design

A 6-character collection was developed:

- Hala (the baby Saker Falcon) — the anchor character
- 5 supporting characters in Gulf-heritage themes (desert, marine, mountain, gold-souk, modern UAE skyline)
- Each available as plush keychain, vinyl figure, and blind-box edition

### 3. Manufacturer Sourcing & Production

Working with our China-side designer toy supply network (Daimio, Maytree, and a Foshan plush-specialist factory), we produced:

- 6 character vinyl figures (PVC, ~10–12 cm)
- 6 character plush keychains (soft-touch fabric)
- Blind-box packaging with magnetic close, premium printing
- Custom retail display POS units for Magic Planet kiosks

Production lead time: **6 weeks** from final approved artwork.

### 4. ESMA Conformity

Children's product safety was non-negotiable for a mall-traffic retail environment. We submitted samples to an approved UAE lab for **EQM (Emirates Quality Mark)** testing:

- EN 71 mechanical safety
- ISO 8124 small-parts choking-hazard testing
- Phthalate / heavy-metal screening
- Manufacturer dossier review

Certificate issued in **3 weeks**, before first retail shipment.

### 5. Customs & VAT Clearance

Import flowed through Jebel Ali Port (DP World) under **HS 9503** classification:

- 5% UAE Federal Customs duty on FOB value (handled via Mirsal-2 e-clearance)
- 5% UAE VAT on CIF + duty (claimable input VAT via MUSE's FTA-registered TRN)
- 7 days customs clearance for first shipment

For more on UAE import flow, see our [Designer Toys Import Guide](/blog/importing-designer-toys-to-uae-2026-guide/).

### 6. GCC Retail Rollout

Distribution flowed from MUSE Dubai warehouse to Magic Planet retail kiosks:

- UAE: 2-day delivery (Aramex B2B)
- Saudi Arabia / Oman / Bahrain Magic Planet locations: 3–5 day delivery

Stock replenishment was handled monthly based on Magic Planet's retail POS data — MUSE Dubai inventory feeds 6 countries with **same-week reorder turnaround**.

## Outcome

- **First shipment delivered** within 12 weeks of project kickoff (concept → shelf)
- **6-country GCC rollout** across Magic Planet network
- **Multi-year IP licensing** framework established for future Hala collection expansions
- **MUSE's Hala IP** moved from concept to revenue-generating retail SKU set in a single year

## Why It Worked

**1. Pre-cleared compliance + ready logistics.** MUSE's Dubai warehouse, MoEC-recognised VAT TRN, and pre-clearance customs broker meant Magic Planet didn't have to learn the import/conformity process. MUSE absorbed it.

**2. Original IP, not borrowed brand.** Magic Planet didn't want a Pop Mart or Disney character (those are price-pressured and contract-locked). Hala gave the partnership clean IP ownership and brand alignment with UAE heritage.

**3. China supply chain access.** MUSE's direct relationships with top Chinese designer-toy manufacturers (with [Pop Mart](https://www.popmart.com)–trained engineering talent) meant production quality and price-per-unit hit the right zone for mall-retail margins.
